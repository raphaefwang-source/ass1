# LJ-A: can dt = 0.01 be used to show VACF, VCCF, C_L / C_T and the distinct van Hove function?

Scope: LJ, law A, N = 256, ρ* = 0.75, k_BT = 1. The model, PPPM set and full-periodic convention are unchanged.

This is a supplementary analysis of the existing trajectories; no new long trajectories were run. Every earlier verdict
in `../REPORT.md` stands unchanged, in particular:
- no dt validated under the pre-registered rule;
- RDF norm INCONCLUSIVE;
- MSD(5) FAIL at 0.0025;
- long-time D plateau not confirmed.

All numbers below come from `results.json`, which is generated by `lj075_observable_dt.py analyze`, unless another file
is named. A few derived numbers were computed for this revision from the per-replica values in `results.json` or from
`per_replica_observables.npz`; they are marked "(derived)". The full cell tables are in `conclusions_table.md`
(protocol v3) and `conclusions_table_v2.md` (protocol v2).

## Answers

**A. What dt = 0.01 supports for display (1% of each feature's own amplitude; simultaneous 97.5% bounds for the
difference and for the scale, jointly ≥ 95%)**

Nothing of substance can be certified, and the same holds for dt = 0.005:
- Of 71 observable × window cells, one is SUPPORTED: the distinct van Hove core window (r < 0.9) at t = 0.1. It is
  close to vacuous:
  - The window holds a single r bin (r = 0.84). Bins with r ≤ 0.79 are excluded by the count rule (fewer than 100
    reference pairs per replica; g_d ≈ 0 there at every dt).
  - In that bin the reference g_d is 0.012. The 1% tolerance is taken against the depth of the g_d − 1 hole
    (S = 0.988), i.e. 0.0099 g units, about 85% of the local value. The measured bound, |Δg_d| ≤ 9.1e-4, is about 8%
    of the local g_d.
  - It is not support for displaying the van Hove core region or its structure: at t = 0.1 the first-shell window
    (0.9 < r < 1.59) is INCONCLUSIVE (E_sig upper 5.1%) and r > 1.59 meets unresolved lobes. The same applies to
    dt = 0.005. Apart from this edge bin, no van Hove or time-correlation cell is SUPPORTED at 0.01 or 0.005.
- 5 cells are NOT SUPPORTED (`display_support`), all because of FAILs (item B):
  - VCCF far shell (r > 2.55), t ∈ (0.2, 0.5]: FAIL in the primary and in the secondary group;
  - VCCF far shell, whole curve: secondary FAIL;
  - C_L(k1), t ∈ (0.5, 1] and whole curve: secondary FAIL;
  - C_L(k2), t ∈ (1, 2]: secondary FAIL.
- The other 65 cells are INCONCLUSIVE. Under protocol v2 the split (SUPPORTED / NOT SUPPORTED / INCONCLUSIVE) is
  1 / 4 / 66 (see History).
- **Absolute units.** A display in absolute units also needs vacf_C0, CL0 and CT0 to PASS
  (RULES.decision.absolute_units). At 0.01 only vacf_C0 PASSes; all six CL0 / CT0 scalars are INCONCLUSIVE (e.g. CT0(k1)
  +2.85% [−0.97, +6.66]). So an absolute-unit C_L / C_T display is not supported either; only the normalized (shape)
  display was assessed.

Most cells are undecided because the bands are too wide (item B). That is not evidence of no dt effect: several
deviations at dt = 0.01 are resolved (item B). On the decidable side:
- **Amplitude.** dt = 0.01 lowers ⟨|v|²⟩, i.e. T_kin, by −0.38% [−0.67, −0.09] (PASS at 1%, deviation resolved). The
  secondary 0.01 vs 0.005 gives −0.34% [−0.58, −0.11] (PASS, resolved); 0.005 and 0.0025 vs 0.00125 give −0.05% and
  −0.04% (PASS).
- **Static positions.** The first-peak positions of g_d(r,t) at t = 0.1, 0.5 and 1 PASS at 0.01 vs 0.00125.
- **Secondary group (0.01 vs 0.005 only, 11 long chains).** These three scalars PASS. They are agreements between 0.01
  and 0.005 only: against 0.00125 all three are INCONCLUSIVE at 0.01, and the secondary group can only veto display
  support, not give it.
  - D_GK to t = 2: absolute +1.6e-4 [−2.3e-4, +5.5e-4], with D_GK ≈ 0.0294. It PASSes on the frozen protocol scale for
    integrals, (1/3)∫₀²|C|dt = 0.0674 ≈ 2.3 × D_GK (the negative VACF lobe is counted with a plus sign); on that scale
    the change is +0.24% [−0.34, +0.82]. Relative to D_GK itself it is +0.55% [−0.78, +1.88] (derived), so agreement
    of D_GK within 1% of its own value is not shown.
  - VACF zero crossing: +0.07% [−0.34, +0.48].
  - g_d first-peak excess (g_d − 1) at t = 0.1: +0.05% [−0.38, +0.48].

## Definitions and protocol (`protocol.json`; v2 as frozen: `protocol_v2_frozen.json`)

**Observables.** These are the project definitions of `prl_toy_models.compute_observables`. My implementation
reproduces them to ≤ 1e-15 on a real trajectory. The check was rerun for this revision and saved as
`definition_check.json`: replica 101, dt 0.01, origins t₀ = 1, 2, 3, all lags ≤ 2; max |difference| 8.9e-16 for the
VACF, ≤ 4.4e-16 for VCCF, C_L and C_T, and 0 for g_d.
- **VACF** Z(t) = ⟨v_i(t₀)·v_i(t₀+t)⟩/⟨|v|²⟩.
- **VCCF** C_b(t) = ⟨v_i(t₀)·v_j(t₀+t)⟩/⟨|v|²⟩ over ordered pairs with r_ij(t₀) in [0, 1.59), [1.59, 2.55) or
  [2.55, L/2). The edges are the first and second minima of the reference g(r).
- **C_L, C_T** use j(k,t) = N⁻¹ Σ v_i e^{ik·r_i}. Each mode is normalized by its own t = 0 value, then averaged over
  the shell. Shells: |k| = 0.899 (k1: 3 modes), 1.271 (k2: 6 modes), 1.798 (k4: 3 modes).
- **van Hove** g_d(r,t) = V/(n₀N(N−1)) Σ_{i≠j} δ(r − |r_j(t₀+t) − r_i(t₀)|)/(4πr²dr) at t = 0.1, 0.5, 1 and 2.
  - With N(N−1) ordered pairs, g_d(r,0) = g(r) and g_d → 1 for uncorrelated pairs.
  - g_d(r,0) is plotted only; it is not judged.
  - r bins with fewer than 100 reference pairs per replica are excluded from bands and scales
    (`protocol.json` derived.*.van_hove.*.excluded_bins; e.g. r ≤ 0.79 at t = 0.1).
- **Zero total momentum** gives the sum rule Σ_{j≠i} v_i·v_j(t) = −v_i·v_i(t). The mean over all pairs is −Z/(N−1).
  - This is not a per-bin baseline: the bins cover only r < L/2, so nothing is subtracted.
  - If velocities are independent of positions at t₀, every bin starts at C_b(0) = −1/(N−1) = −0.00392. The measured
    values (replica means, derived from `per_replica_scalars`; replica SE 0.00004–0.00044) are:

    | group, shell | 0.00125 | 0.0025 | 0.005 | 0.01 |
    |---|---|---|---|---|
    | primary, first | −0.00426 | −0.00386 | −0.00379 | −0.00339 |
    | primary, second | −0.00437 | −0.00397 | −0.00390 | −0.00389 |
    | primary, far | −0.00375 | −0.00385 | −0.00391 | −0.00396 |
    | secondary, first | – | – | −0.00386 | −0.00323 |
    | secondary, second | – | – | −0.00400 | −0.00390 |
    | secondary, far | – | – | −0.00393 | −0.00402 |

  - Three values lie more than 2 replica-SE from −1/(N−1): the primary 0.00125 second shell (−2.5 SE), the secondary
    0.01 first shell (+3.0 SE) and the secondary 0.01 far shell (−2.3 SE).
  - The secondary first-shell difference 0.01 − 0.005 is resolved: +6.4e-4 [+0.7e-4, +12.1e-4]. The primary
    0.01 vs 0.00125 points the same way: +8.7e-4 [−2.8e-4, +2.0e-3]. This bears on the protocol's assumption that
    velocities are independent of positions at t₀.
  - The VCCF is divided by ⟨|v|²⟩, not by C_b(0), so such offsets stay in the curve differences (see the second-shell
    FAIL in item B).
- **Normalization.** Curves are normalized by each level's own t = 0 value, so verdicts are shape-only. Amplitude
  (T_kin) effects are judged by the scalars vacf_C0, CL0 and CT0.

**Common rules.**
- **Grid and origins.** Every level is read at the same physical times: grid 0.01, lags ≤ 2. Origins are t₀ ∈ [1, 8]
  in steps of 0.05 (primary) and [10, 98] in steps of 0.25 (secondary).
- **Statistical unit.** The unit is the independent replica, with differences paired per replica. Origins,
  particles, frames and bins are averaged inside a replica.

**Error definition.**
- The reference mean curve is cut into sign lobes. Each point is judged against the amplitude of its own lobe, so
  weak lobes are judged against their own size and no relative error is formed at zero crossings.
- E_sig = sup over the window of |mean paired difference| / lobe amplitude. E_abs, in the displayed units, is always
  reported alongside.
- **Bands.** The difference band is simultaneous at 97.5% over the window. It uses c = max of a re-studentized
  sign-flip max-t value (all 2^(n−1) sign vectors) and Bonferroni-t. The lobe amplitude band is simultaneous at 97.5%
  over the curve, so the two hold jointly at ≥ 95%.
- **Self-test** (`band_selftest.json`). It checks the 95% version of the band construction (max of sign-flip max-t
  and Bonferroni-t), with full enumeration at n = 11 and 4096 random sign vectors at n = 16, for p = 20–200, over 1000
  synthetic Gaussian repetitions. Worst coverage is 0.94 (nominal 0.95; binomial s.e. about 0.007), and 0.98–0.99 for
  smooth correlated columns. The analysis uses 97.5% with full 2^15 enumeration, and some of its van Hove windows have
  only p = 1–12 points; those settings were not tested. Since c ≥ Bonferroni-t, the band is exact or conservative under per-point
  normality there, but that rests on the normality assumption, not on the self-test.
- **Pointwise t-intervals** appear only in the figures (shaded) and are never used for a verdict.

**Scalar scales.** Relative scalar differences are fractions of |mean reference| for values, times and positions, of
mean(peak) − 1 for van Hove peak heights, and of ∫₀²|mean reference curve|dt for integrals. For D_GK this integral
scale is (1/3)∫₀²|C|dt, which is 2.3 × D_GK (secondary) and 2.4 × D_GK (primary), so a percentage on it is not a
percentage of D.

**Verdicts.**
- **Curve windows:** PASS if the upper bound ≤ 1% and every lobe in the window is resolved (relative half-width ≤ 10%);
  FAIL if the lower bound > 1%; otherwise INCONCLUSIVE.
- **Scalars:** paired t-intervals (97.5%), with the absolute interval always reported. The relative verdict needs a
  resolved reference. A scalar undefined in any replica is INCONCLUSIVE; no replica is dropped.
- **Display support:** dt is SUPPORTED in a cell only if it PASSes vs 0.00125 and 0.0025 vs 0.00125 also PASSes
  there. A FAIL in the primary, in the secondary or in a shape scalar gives NOT SUPPORTED. E_glob (a fraction of the
  global maximum) is descriptive only.

**History.**
- The protocol was reviewed independently before freezing and redesigned after the review (commit 4472aa0: new band
  construction, per-lobe scales, scalar rules). The review itself is not saved as a file.
- **v3 amendment.** It was made after the v2 freeze and before any difference was computed. Every lobe is now judged
  on its own instead of merging everything after the first unresolved lobe into one tail.
  - Reason: under v2, the C_b(0) = −1/(N−1) transient hid the resolved first-shell VCCF peak.
  - Effect on lobes: the first-shell VCCF peak lobe becomes resolved in both groups. The secondary g_d(r, t = 2) also
    gains two resolved lobes (r 1.49–1.89 and 1.95–2.36). The amendment text frozen in `protocol.json` ("only that
    peak lobe changes status") misses the latter; `protocol.json` is hash-protected and is not edited.
  - Effect on verdicts: v2 and v3 differ in 2 of 284 cells, both INCONCLUSIVE → FAIL: primary VCCF far shell at 0.01,
    t ∈ (0.2, 0.5], and secondary VCCF far shell, whole curve. Under v2 the primary group has no 0.01 FAIL, and 0.01
    has 4 NOT SUPPORTED cells instead of 5. The peak lobe that motivated the amendment changed no verdict, and neither
    did the new g_d lobes.
  - The amendment made nothing more lenient: weak unresolved lobes now carry their own small scale, which can only
    make a FAIL easier, never a PASS.
- **Prior looks.** The VACF (scaled by C(0), including the t = 0 value ⟨|v|²⟩, i.e. T_kin) and g(r) differences had

## Minimal follow-up (not run; `followup_plan.json`)

Pairing helps most at the shared start.
- With the single origin t₀ = 0 (identical state), the paired-difference spread for t ≤ 0.5 is smaller than the
  all-origin paired spread of the analysis by 0.7–3.6× at dt = 0.01 and 1.3–5.6× at dt = 0.005 (ratio of the window
  maxima). At dt = 0.01 there is no gain for C_L(k2) (0.73×, i.e. the bursts are noisier) and only 1.2× for C_L(k4).
  Example at dt = 0.01, VACF head lobe t ∈ [0.01, 0.11]: 0.0016 vs 0.0050; dip lobe t ∈ [0.12, 0.5]: 0.0026 vs 0.0061.
- For t > 0.5 the burst gain is gone (VACF in (0.5, 1]: ratio 0.98 at dt = 0.01).
- With origins t₀ ∈ [1, 8], pairing still helps, but less. The paired spread for t ≤ 0.5 is about 1.3× (VACF), 2.1×
  (C_L(k1)) and 2.3× (C_T(k1)) below the unpaired √(var_a + var_b) at dt = 0.01 (median over t; derived from
  `per_replica_observables.npz`).

**Plan.**
- **Design:** about 170 independent canonical starts (Langevin-prepared, 13 s each), each a coupled four-level burst
  (0.00125–0.01) of 0.51 time units. Frames every step, origin t₀ = 0 only.
- **Cost:** about 0.04 core-h per burst, about 6.8 core-h in total (about 1.7 h on 4 cores). This uses the measured
  step time of the existing chains with ranks 12/5 (0.173 s per level step). With the recommended 14/5 ranks, add
  9–13% (`timing.json`).
- **Bursts needed** for a 1%-of-lobe decision for dt = 0.01. The first column assumes a true difference of 0. The
  second assumes the pilot mean difference, i.e. the maximum of |mean| over the window from 16 bursts. That maximum is
  inflated by noise, so the second column is a pessimistic bound, not evidence of a difference above 1%. Where the
  pilot value exceeds the tolerance (VACF dip 9.1e-4 vs 6.9e-4; C_L(k4) 0.012 vs 0.0088), it is the count for a FAIL
  decision.

  | target | bursts, true difference 0 | bursts, at the pilot difference |
  |---|---|---|
  | VACF head | 4 | 5 |
  | C_T first lobes (k1, k2, k4) | 31, 14, 38 | 124, 29, 752 |
  | C_L(k1) | 20 | 73 |
  | VCCF first-shell peak | 54 | 619 |
  | C_L(k2, k4) | 167, 126 | 752, 998 |
  | VACF dip | 167 | 1647 (about 67 core-h) |

- **Limits of the sizing.**
  - It divides by the lobe amplitude S, while PASS divides by its lower bound S_lo. For the VACF dip this makes the
    counts optimistic by (S/S_lo)² ≈ 1.24 (about 207 instead of 167 bursts).
  - It ignores the lobe-resolution requirement. The primary VACF dip lobe is unresolved (relative half-width
    0.104 > 0.1), so the dip cannot PASS at any burst count unless its reference lobe is resolved as well.
  - Bursts from t₀ = 0 start from an exact canonical state. They measure the difference that develops from that
    state, not the stationary difference seen at origins t₀ ∈ [1, 8] (e.g. T_kin −0.38% there).
- **What it does not decide.** The VACF in (0.5, 1] needs ≥ 700 bursts (about 55 core-h; about 880 with S_lo) at a
  true difference of 0. (1, 2] is not reachable this way: more than 5000 bursts at a true difference of 0, because the
  burst spread is 3.5× the all-origin spread there. Those windows, and the long-time D plateau, stay unresolved. The
  t ≤ 2 results here say nothing about the long-time D.
