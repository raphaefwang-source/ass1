# LJ-A: can dt = 0.01 be used to show VACF, VCCF, C_L / C_T and the distinct van Hove function?

Scope: LJ, law A, N = 256, ρ* = 0.75, k_BT = 1. The model, PPPM set and full-periodic convention are unchanged.

This is a supplementary analysis of the existing trajectories; no new long trajectories were run. Every earlier verdict
in `../REPORT.md` stands unchanged, in particular:
- no dt validated under the pre-registered rule;
- RDF norm INCONCLUSIVE;
- MSD(5) FAIL at 0.0025;
- long-time D plateau not confirmed.

All numbers below come from `results.json`, which is generated by `lj075_observable_dt.py analyze`. The full cell
tables are in `conclusions_table.md` (protocol v3) and `conclusions_table_v2.md` (protocol v2).

## Answers

**A. What dt = 0.01 supports for display (1% of each feature's own amplitude, simultaneous 95% bounds)**

Hardly anything can be certified, and the same holds for dt = 0.005:
- Of 71 observable × window cells, only the distinct van Hove core (r < 0.9) at t = 0.1 is SUPPORTED.
- 5 cells are NOT SUPPORTED, all isolated FAILs (item B).
- The rest are INCONCLUSIVE.

This is a resolution limit, not an observed dt effect. On the decidable side:
- **Amplitude.** dt = 0.01 lowers ⟨|v|²⟩, i.e. T_kin, by −0.38% [−0.67, −0.09] (PASS, resolved).
- **Static positions.** The first-peak positions of g_d(r,t) at t = 0.1, 0.5 and 1 PASS.
- **Secondary group (0.01 vs 0.005, 11 long chains).** These PASS:
  - D_GK to t = 2: +0.24% [−0.34, +0.82];
  - the VACF zero crossing;
  - the g_d first-peak height at t = 0.1.

**B. Clear deviations versus error bars that are too wide**

- **No systematic dt dependence is detected.**
  - For every observable, the whole-curve difference 0.01 − 0.00125 is about the size of 0.0025 − 0.00125, which is
    the noise floor. Examples: VACF 3.3e-3 vs 2.2e-3 of C(0); C_L(k1) 0.024 vs 0.024; VCCF first shell 9.1e-4 vs
    6.4e-4 of ⟨|v|²⟩.
  - No whole-curve (0 < t ≤ 2) deviation is resolved in the primary group.
- **The 5 FAIL cells are isolated and non-systematic.**
  - Primary: VCCF far shell (r > 2.55) at 0.01, t ∈ (0.2, 0.5]; VCCF second shell at 0.005, t ≤ 0.2.
  - Secondary 0.01 vs 0.005: VCCF far shell, t ∈ (0.2, 0.5]; C_L(k1), t ∈ (0.5, 1]; C_L(k2), t ∈ (1, 2].
  - Their paired differences change sign inside the window. The "repeated" far-shell FAIL has opposite signs in the
    two groups (−2e-4 vs +1e-4 of ⟨|v|²⟩).
  - The count, 5 of 224 window cells, matches the rate expected by chance: at most 2.5% per cell from the 97.5%
    bands, i.e. about 5.6.
  - They are reported as observed and keep the NOT SUPPORTED label.
- **One scalar shows a resolved shift.** The C_T(k1) half-decay time differs by +3.6% [+0.6, +6.6] at 0.01 vs
  0.00125. It is not seen in 0.01 vs 0.005 (+0.25% [−1.8, +2.3]) and is treated as unconfirmed.
- **Everything else is INCONCLUSIVE because the error bars are too wide.** There are two causes:
  1. **The reference signal of weak lobes is not resolved to ±10%** (I* cells): the VACF dip in the primary group
     (relative half-width 0.104 > 0.1), the negative lobes of C_L / C_T, the second- and third-shell VCCF, and the
     van Hove tails. Here no relative 1% verdict is possible.
  2. **The difference band is wider than 1% of the lobe**, even where the lobe is resolved. Upper bounds are 3–15%
     for VACF, C_T and van Hove first-shell cells, e.g. C_T(k1) for t ≤ 0.2: E_sig 2.2% [0.1, 4.2].

**C. Run-time saving of dt = 0.01 over 0.005 for the same physical time**

| measure | saving per physical time |
|---|---|
| measured (`timing.json`, interleaved production-runner runs) | 48.7% (ranks 12/5); 50.4% (ranks 14/5) |
| step-count estimate | 50% |

- The per-step cost is independent of dt: 0.183 vs 0.178 s (12/5) and 0.199 vs 0.201 s (14/5), for 0.01 vs 0.005.
- The saving is therefore exactly the halved step count.

## Definitions and protocol (`protocol.json`; v2 as frozen: `protocol_v2_frozen.json`)

**Observables.** These are the project definitions of `prl_toy_models.compute_observables`; my implementation
reproduces them to ≤ 1e-15 on a real trajectory.
- **VACF** Z(t) = ⟨v_i(t₀)·v_i(t₀+t)⟩/⟨|v|²⟩.
- **VCCF** C_b(t) = ⟨v_i(t₀)·v_j(t₀+t)⟩/⟨|v|²⟩ over ordered pairs with r_ij(t₀) in [0, 1.59), [1.59, 2.55) or
  [2.55, L/2). The edges are the first and second minima of the reference g(r).
- **C_L, C_T** use j(k,t) = N⁻¹ Σ v_i e^{ik·r_i}. Each mode is normalized by its own t = 0 value, then averaged over
  the shell. Shells: |k| = 0.899 (k1: 3 modes), 1.271 (k2: 6 modes), 1.798 (k4: 3 modes).
- **van Hove** g_d(r,t) = V/(n₀N(N−1)) Σ_{i≠j} δ(r − |r_j(t₀+t) − r_i(t₀)|)/(4πr²dr) at t = 0.1, 0.5, 1 and 2.
  - With N(N−1) ordered pairs, g_d(r,0) = g(r) and g_d → 1 for uncorrelated pairs.
  - g_d(r,0) is plotted only; it is not judged.
- **Zero total momentum** gives the sum rule Σ_{j≠i} v_i·v_j(t) = −v_i·v_i(t). The mean over all pairs is −Z/(N−1).
  - This is not a per-bin baseline: the bins cover only r < L/2, so nothing is subtracted.
  - All three bins start at C_b(0) ≈ −1/(N−1) = −0.0039, as they should.
- **Normalization.** Curves are normalized by each level's own t = 0 value, so verdicts are shape-only. Amplitude
  (T_kin) effects are judged by the scalars vacf_C0, CL0 and CT0.

**Common rules.**
- **Grid and origins.** Every level is read at the same physical times: grid 0.01, lags ≤ 2. Origins are t₀ ∈ [1, 8]
  in steps of 0.05 (primary) and [10, 98] in steps of 0.25 (secondary).
- **Statistical unit.** The unit is the independent replica, with differences paired per replica. Origins,
  particles, frames and bins are averaged inside a replica.

**Error definition.**
- The reference mean curve is cut into sign lobes. Each point is judged against the amplitude of its own lobe, so
  weak lobes are judged against their own size and no relative error is formed at zero crossings.
- E_sig = sup over the window of |mean paired difference| / lobe amplitude. E_abs, in the displayed units, is always
  reported alongside.
- **Bands.** The difference band is simultaneous at 97.5% over the window. It uses c = max of a re-studentized
  sign-flip max-t value (all 2^(n−1) sign vectors) and Bonferroni-t. The lobe amplitude band is simultaneous at 97.5%
  over the curve, so the two hold jointly at ≥ 95%.
- **Self-test** (`band_selftest.json`): worst coverage of the band used is 0.94, nominal 0.95, over 1000 synthetic
  repetitions at n = 11 and 16 and p = 20–200.
- **Pointwise t-intervals** appear only in the figures (shaded) and are never used for a verdict.

**Verdicts.**
- **Curve windows:** PASS if the upper bound ≤ 1% and every lobe in the window is resolved (relative half-width ≤ 10%);
  FAIL if the lower bound > 1%; otherwise INCONCLUSIVE.
- **Scalars:** paired t-intervals (97.5%), with the absolute interval always reported. The relative verdict needs a
  resolved reference. A scalar undefined in any replica is INCONCLUSIVE; no replica is dropped.
- **Display support:** dt is SUPPORTED in a cell only if it PASSes vs 0.00125 and 0.0025 vs 0.00125 also PASSes
  there. A FAIL in the primary, in the secondary or in a shape scalar gives NOT SUPPORTED. E_glob (a fraction of the
  global maximum) is descriptive only.

**History.**
- The protocol was reviewed independently before freezing. The review found that the first band design covered only
  0.3–0.9, among 27 confirmed issues, and all were fixed.
- **v3 amendment.** It was made after the v2 freeze and before any difference was computed. Every lobe is now judged
  on its own instead of merging everything after the first unresolved lobe into one tail.
  - Reason: under v2, the C_b(0) = −1/(N−1) transient hid the resolved first-shell VCCF peak.
  - Effect: v2 and v3 differ in 2 of 284 cells, both INCONCLUSIVE → FAIL. The amendment made nothing more lenient.
- **Prior looks.** The VACF (scaled by C(0)) and g(r) differences had been seen in the earlier dt study, so they are
  not blind.

## Minimal follow-up (not run; `followup_plan.json`)

Pairing helps only at the shared start: the velocities of the coupled levels decorrelate within about 1 time unit.
- With origins t₀ ∈ [1, 8] (or [1, 1.2]), the paired-difference spread is the same as for unpaired runs.
- With the single origin t₀ = 0 (identical state), it is 3–5× smaller for t ≤ 0.5. For example, VACF lag 0.1–0.3:
  0.0015 vs 0.005.

**Plan.**
- **Design:** about 170 independent canonical starts (Langevin-prepared, 13 s each), each a coupled four-level burst
  (0.00125–0.01) of 0.51 time units. Frames every step, origin t₀ = 0 only, ranks as for production.
- **Cost:** about 0.04 core-h per burst, about 6.8 core-h in total (about 1.7 h on 4 cores).
- **Bursts needed** for a 1%-of-lobe decision for dt = 0.01, at a true difference of 0 (optimistic; more if the true
  difference is near 1%):

  | target | bursts |
  |---|---|
  | VACF head | 4 |
  | C_T first lobes (k1, k2, k4) | 31, 14, 38 |
  | C_L(k1) | 20 |
  | VCCF first-shell peak | 54 |
  | C_L(k2, k4) | 167, 126 |
  | VACF dip | 167 |

- **What it does not decide.** For t > 0.5 the pairing gain is gone. The VACF in (0.5, 1] needs ≥ 700 bursts
  (about 55 core-h), and (1, 2] is not reachable this way. Those windows, and the long-time D plateau, stay unresolved.
  The t ≤ 2 results here say nothing about the long-time D.
