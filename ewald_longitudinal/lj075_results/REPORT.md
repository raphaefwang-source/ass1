## 2. Operator and Lanczos accuracy

### 2.1 Error definitions

- **PPPM operator error.** The spectral relative error ε_op = ‖Γ_h − Γ_ref‖₂ / ‖Γ_ref‖₂.
  - Γ_ref is the full periodic lattice-sum friction matrix (`LatticeFriction`) with the k = 0 term retained. Near
    images are summed directly and the far field by a Chebyshev fit. A brute-force self-check agrees to 9.4e-16 of
    the largest entry, and the KD-tree pair set equals the image pair set.
  - The k = 0 term is exact in both matrices. Its share is N c₀ = ρĝ(0)/3 = 10.35 out of λ_max ≈ 12, so including it
    lowers the relative error. ε_op is therefore also reported against the k ≠ 0 part,
    ‖Γ_h − Γ_ref‖₂ / ‖Γ_ref − c₀(N I − 1 1ᵀ) ⊗ I₃‖₂.
  - Budget 2e-7, applied to the full-norm definition as in the project. The k ≠ 0 value is reported, not used for
    pass/fail; for A it is within the budget as well.
  - Further reported metrics: the error on Gaussian probe vectors, the errors of the lowest 12 eigenvalues, symmetry,
    the momentum null space (Γ·1 = 0 per component, for both Γ_h and Γ_ref), and positivity on the range.
- **Lanczos error.** The relative 2-norm error of f(Γ_h)v against the dense matrix function of the same, fixed Γ_h.
  - Functions: the damping e^{−hΓ/m}, the noise amplitude S(Γ) = √(kT m(1 − e^{−2hΓ/m})), and the two coupling
    maps.
  - Inputs:
    - 4 Gaussian and 4 Maxwell vectors;
    - the actual momentum p' of the step (all canonical and visited states) and the actual noise draw ξ (only the
      equilibration-visited states, whose RNG stream is regenerated);
    - longitudinal and transverse plane waves along (1,0,0), (0,1,0), (0,0,1) (|k| = 2π/L) and (1,1,0)
      (|k| = √2·2π/L);
    - 2 slow-mode-dominated vectors (slowest eigenvector plus 10% noise).
  - Error components, taken as the maximum over all probes: overall; k_min (the error projected on the three
    |k| = 2π/L Fourier modes, relative to the same projection of the exact result); lowest 3 and lowest 12
    eigenmodes.
  - Strict rule: max(overall, k_min, lowest 3, lowest 12) ≤ budget/10 = 2e-8 relative to the vector. Relative to the
    O-step increment only the overall error is checked, against the same limit.
- **Physical-quantity errors** are measured directly (§2.4, §3) and never inferred from ε_op.

## 3. Time step

### 3.1 Method

- **Levels.** dt = 0.01, 0.005, 0.0025 and 0.00125, integrated in lock-step from the same (q₀, p₀) to the same
  physical end time t_end = 10 (`lj075_dt_study.py`, `lj075_coupled.py`).
- **Noise coupling (nested, exact).**
  - The finest level draws ξ ~ N(0, Π). Each coarser level builds its input from two inputs of the next finer level
    with its own Γ_c: ξ_c = a(Γ_c)ξ₁ + b(Γ_c)ξ₂, with a = e^{−x}/√(1+e^{−2x}), b = 1/√(1+e^{−2x}) and x = hΓ_c/m.
  - Because a² + b² = 1, ξ_c is exactly N(0, Π) conditional on the past, so every level samples its own correct law.
    For frozen Γ, one coarse O-step equals two fine ones.
  - Using the same seed alone would not couple the levels.
- **Replicas.** 16 independent canonical starts (`dtinit_s101–112, 116–119`): random sequential addition, then
  30 time units of scalar Langevin (γ = 1) with the conservative forces only. Their mean kinetic T over the last
  quarter of the preparation is 0.995 ± 0.012 (95% CI over the 16 starts).
- **Analysis.** Paired by replica. Time averages over [1, 10]. VACF on the 0.01 grid with all time origins. MSD with
  all origins and the centre of mass removed.

### 3.2 Pre-registered criteria (`dt_criteria.json`, committed before any dt-study run)

- Tolerances:
  - T_kin and U/N: 1% relative;
  - pressure: 0.0075 absolute;
  - RDF: E_g = ‖Δg‖_w / ‖g_ref − 1‖_w ≤ 1%, with w = 4πρr²dr on [0.5, L/2);
  - RDF first-peak height and position: 1% relative;
  - VACF: max_{t ≤ 2} |ΔC|/C_ref(0) ≤ 1% (absolute, normalised by C(0), because the VACF crosses zero);
  - MSD at t = 0.1, 0.5, 1, 2 and 5: 1% relative.
- Verdicts: PASS = the whole 95% interval inside the band; FAIL = the whole interval outside; otherwise
  INCONCLUSIVE.
- Decision: first the finest two levels, then the largest dt whose criteria all PASS.
- Amendment made before any multi-replica analysis: function-valued bounds use Student-t bounds instead of the
  registered percentile bootstrap.
  - VACF: a pointwise-t envelope, which is more conservative than the bootstrap. Its lower bound is the maximum of
    pointwise lower bounds, not a simultaneous bound.
  - RDF norm: a debiased-jackknife bound. This is **less** strict than the registered bootstrap bound, because it
    removes the upward noise bias of the norm. The RDF norm is therefore reported under both, and the registered
    bootstrap verdict is the one used in the decision.
- Secondary (added after an interim look at n = 6): a Bonferroni family-wise verdict over the 36 tests, reported in
  full. Decisions use the primary verdicts.

1. **Decorrelation limits pairing.** The median RMS velocity difference against the 0.00125 path is 0.025, 0.057 and

### 3.4 Decision

- **Under the pre-registered rule no dt is validated at n = 16.**
  - The finest-two comparison (0.0025 vs 0.00125) is not all-PASS: pressure, RDF norm and MSD(2) are INCONCLUSIVE,
    and MSD(5) FAILs.
  - No coarser level passes every criterion either. The RDF norm (registered bound 1.7–1.8%), MSD(t ≥ 0.5),
    pressure and long-time D (§5) are noise-limited at every dt.
- On the criteria that are decidable at n = 16, all three coarse dt PASS: T, U/N, RDF first-peak height and
  position, VACF (t ≤ 2) and MSD(0.1). A rule applied to this subset alone would therefore pick dt = 0.01. That
  subset rule is post hoc and is not used as validation.
- **Provisional recommendation: dt = 0.005.** It is a precautionary choice, not a validated one.
  - At 0.01 the resolved T (−0.52%) and U/N (−0.16%) biases take a visible part of the 1% budget, and the VACF
    difference stands clearly above its noise floor (0.52% vs 0.24%).
  - The MSD(0.5–2) point estimates at 0.01 (+0.47%, +0.87%, +1.80%) are larger than at 0.005 (+0.41%, +0.52%,
    +0.89%), though unresolved.
  - The long-time D comparison between 0.01 and 0.005 is unresolved (§5).
  - dt = 0.01 is acceptable for static quantities (T, U/N, RDF first peak), with the quoted biases.
- `lj_rho0.75_kT1.0_A_prod` therefore keeps `dt_validated = None` and records `dt_provisional = 0.005`.
- **Remedy under the registered rule: more replicas.** From the observed spread and mean at 0.005, bringing the
  interval inside ±1% needs ≈ 25 replicas for MSD(0.5) and ≈ 80 for MSD(1). For MSD(2) and MSD(5) a 1% verdict is
  infeasible (thousands of replicas).

## 4. Equilibration and burn-in

- **Runs.** 8 runs with the production runner `toy_run.py`: config `lj_rho0.75_kT1.0_costopt`, law A, dt 0.005,
  50 time units each, frames every step, seeds 301–308 (2.9 core-h). Starts:
  - fcc lattice with 0.05 jitter (s301, s302);
  - RSA packing (s303, s304);
  - canonical Langevin state (s305, s306);
  - canonical positions with momenta at kT = 2 (s307, s308).
- **Burn-in rule** (stated before the analysis): stationary pool t ≥ 25; 2-unit block means per start type; burn-in =
  the end of the last block deviating by more than 4 s_B/√n_g from the pool.
  - The observables checked are U/N, T_kin, pressure, RDF first-peak height, S(k_min), 27-cell count variance and the
    RDF distance from the pool. Together they cover energy, RDF and density structure.
- **Result:** 2.0 time units. It is set by:
  - the RSA starts through U/N, T_kin, pressure, RDF peak and RDF distance;
  - the fcc starts through U/N, pressure, cell variance and RDF distance;
  - the hot starts through T_kin.
  The canonical starts need none.
- **MSER** (information only, per run) mostly gives ≤ 2.5. Exceptions:
  - 3.5 for U/N, T_kin and pressure of eq_A_rsa_s304, and 3.0 for the RDF peak of s303;
  - isolated S(k_min) and RDF-distance values of 9.5–16.5.
  All main-observable values stay at or below the recommended 5-unit discard.
- **After burn-in:**
  - start-type dependence is not detected: one-way ANOVA p = 0.21–0.78 for U/N, T, P, RDF peak, S(k_min) and cell
    variance. With 2 runs per start type the test has low power. The largest start-type spread is pressure 0.033
    (fcc 0.790 vs hot 0.757) against the 0.0075 target, and S(k_min) fcc 0.075 vs lgv 0.091. Initial-state
    independence is therefore under-sampled, not verified, and production should start from canonical
    (Langevin-prepared) states;
  - the two halves of the pool agree (paired p ≥ 0.07);
  - the slowest observable, S(k_min), has τ_int ≈ 0.4–0.9, about 50× shorter than a run, so all τ estimates are
    reliable.
- **Recommendation:** discard 5 time units (2.5× the measured value; 0.04 core-h).

0.018 vs 0.021. With 8 runs the sd itself is uncertain by about ±25%.

## 5. Diffusion coefficient

Two independent data sets, analysed separately (§5.1, §5.2). Short-time agreement in §3 is not used as evidence
for the long-time D.

Plateau rule (stated before the long runs were analysed):
- Paired per-run relative change of D between consecutive GK upper limits or consecutive MSD windows.
- "plateau": the whole 95% CI lies within ±2%.
- "drift": the whole CI lies beyond ±2%.
- "undetermined": neither.
- Long-time D counts as determined only if every step from some limit up to the longest one is "plateau".

### 5.1 GK upper limit and MSD window (8 equilibration runs, dt 0.005, 48 time units after burn-in)

- D_MSD: [1,2] 0.0293, [2,4] 0.0285, [4,8] 0.0285, [8,16] 0.0285. The window steps are "undetermined"
  ([2,4] → [4,8]: 0.0% [−2.2, +2.2]).

### 5.3 Conclusion on D

- **The long-time D is not determined at the ±2% level.**
  - D_MSD decreases by about 2% and then 1% per window doubling up to [4, 8]; both decreases are resolved (their CIs
    exclude 0).
  - Beyond that, the changes are within about ±1% but have CIs of ±1.4–2.6%, so no confirmed plateau extends to the
    longest window.
  - The best current estimate is D ≈ 0.0284–0.0286 (dt 0.005, windows [4,8]–[16,32], each ±1.5–2.5%). It should
    be read as an estimate, not as a converged long-time value.
- **dt dependence.**
  - D(0.01)/D(0.005) − 1 is resolved only in the shortest window (+0.8% [+0.05, +1.55]).
  - In the long windows it is within ±1.3% but the CIs are ±2–5%, so the long-time dt effect is under-sampled.
  - The free-particle prior x coth x − 1 (+0.1% at dt 0.01 relative to dt 0.005) is far smaller than the
    resolution reached.
  - The paths at the two dt decorrelate by t ≈ 2, so the long windows are effectively unpaired.
- **Requirement.** The sd of the per-run change [8,16] → [16,32] is ≈ 3.9%. Confirming a ±2% plateau there needs
  ≈ 16 runs of 100 time units at one dt if the true change is 0, and ≈ 60 at the observed +1%. That is ≈ 12–45
  core-h at dt 0.005.
- **Cost of this study:** 17.6 core-h (11 coupled two-level chains).

## 6. Save interval

- **Method.** Offline thinning of the 8 every-step equilibration trajectories (dt 0.005, after burn-in), against the
  every-step reference of the same trajectory (`sampling.json`). The every-step trapezoid GK equals this chain's own
  long-time MSD diffusion, so the reference has no quadrature error relative to the simulated dynamics.
- **Rule** (existing project rule, applied to every run): GK quadrature bias ≤ 0.2%, ≥ 5 stored lags before
  C/C(0) = ½, and τ½ within 2%. Added here: MSD(t) and D_MSD[2, 8] with production-like origins within ±0.2%
  (95% CI).

| Δ | max GK bias | lags before ½ | τ½ | MSD rule | VACF/GK rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.005 (every step) | 0 | 10 | — | PASS | PASS |
| 0.01 | 0.23% | 5 | +0.17% | PASS | FAIL |
| 0.02 | 1.13% | 3 | +0.67% | PASS | FAIL |
| 0.05 | 7.6% | 1 | +0.25% | PASS | FAIL |
| 0.1 | 34% | 1 | +18% | PASS | FAIL |

- The VACF has τ½ = 0.045 time units (9 steps) and a negative minimum at t ≈ 0.17.
- **VACF/GK** therefore needs every step at dt 0.005, or an on-the-fly VACF.
- **MSD and MSD-based D** are unaffected up to Δ = 0.1: within ±0.1% (95% CI).
- Storage at every step is ≈ 1.2 MB per time unit (float32 q and v).
