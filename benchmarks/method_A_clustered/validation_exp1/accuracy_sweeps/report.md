## 1. Model, operator, references

**Model:**
- K_κ(r) = c e^{−κr}/r · r̂r̂ᵀ with **c = γ₀ℓ₀ = 1 exactly**.
- (ΓX)_i = Σ_{j≠i} Σ_n K(q_i − q_j + nL)(X_i − X_j).
  - Full periodic lattice, Fourier k = 0 retained.
  - Self-particle edges excluded; no minimum image.

**c = 1 audit** (`results/smoke/smoke_checks.json`):
- `RadialEwald.amp` = 1.
- θ_full and the direct-sum profile `g_phys` reproduce e^{−κr}/r to 0 ulp.
- `LatticeFriction` uses γ = e^{−κ}, r_ref = 1. Its amplitude γ r_ref e^{κ r_ref} − 1 = 0, and its profile differs from e^{−κr}/r by ≤ 3.0e-15 relative (for κ = 0.2 … 2).
- The old normalisation c = γ r_ref e^{κ r_ref} is not used anywhere in the new code.

**Operator (Candidate 1, θ = 0):**
- The existing positive sharp Laplace split at s = ξ² (`RadialEwald` law A, amp = 1).
- The existing PPPM (`Mesh`: B-spline order p, FFT, influence K̂_L/|Ŵ|² on |k| ≤ k_c, adjoint gather).
- The existing cached D − H action (`CachedGammaH`, through `ewald_exp_lib.ThetaOp`, which adds nothing at θ = 0).
- Errors are measured on the matrix-free action. Γ_h is also assembled densely and independently: real-space blocks from the same image list, plus the mode-space mesh matrix (`mode_pair_matrix_fast`).
- Matrix-free vs dense action: ≤ 5.2e-14 relative in all 927 rows.
- Small system (N = 128): Γ_h assembled from 3N matrix-free actions agrees with the dense assembly to 1.1e-15 (`smoke_checks.json`).

**Cutoffs:**
- s_R = ξ r_c and s_F = k_c/(2ξ) are the stored sweep parameters; the figures show the Fourier cutoff as u_F = k_c/ξ = 2 s_F.
- k_c is snapped to the largest reciprocal shell 2π√n/L ≤ 2ξ s_F, and the realised s_F is recorded (column `sF_eff`; e.g. s_F = 6 → 5.983, i.e. k_c/ξ = 11.967). The plotted k_c/ξ is 2·`sF_eff`, not a relabelled request.
- Every mesh passes the existing `check_mesh` test (Nyquist m_c < M/2, η < 1, complete shells, no |Ŵ|² ≤ 1e-10). All 927 points are valid.

### Reference: direct periodic summation (Γ_D), validated against LatticeFriction (Γ_LF)

**Construction of Γ_D:**
- Unsplit direct image sum Σ_{|d|≤R_D} K(d) with exactly g = e^{−κr}/r. It uses `direct_periodic.annulus_tensors`, the existing convergence-test form of `DirectPairs`: same images, accumulated per annulus, so Γ_D(R) is available on a radius grid.
- The result is assembled as a graph Laplacian.
- `DirectPairs` itself was cross-checked against it to 3.2e-16 (smoke).
- `direct_periodic.py` received a backward-compatible optional `g=` argument; its default behaviour is unchanged.

**Choice of R_D:**
- Per (κ, ρ, configuration), R_D is the smallest R on a 2.5 grid whose certified bound satisfies 2·`tail_bound_per_particle`(R)/‖Γ‖₂ ≤ 1e-13. This bound is a summation-by-parts bound with a conservative image-count majorant, rigorous for decreasing g.
- R = 55 suffices only for κ ≥ 0.7. Weak screening needs R_D = 190 (κ = 0.2, 2.75e9 images per configuration, 440 s).

**Independent check:** `LatticeFriction` (near images direct, Chebyshev far field) at rel_tol 1e-10, 1e-12, 1e-14.

**Cache:** `raw/exp_refs_c1/<config>_rho<ρ>_kap<κ>_c1_L<L>_<position-hash>.npz` (git-ignored, 72 files, 649 MB). The key contains κ, c, ρ, L, the configuration name and the position hash, so nothing is reused across physical parameters.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

- The two independent references converge to each other as LatticeFriction's tolerance is tightened. At 1e-14 they agree to **≤ 3.3e-15** for every κ, ρ and configuration.
- The **reference floor** used below is ‖Γ_LF(1e-14) − Γ_D‖/‖Γ_D‖ (column `ref_floor_G`). It is 0.6–3.3e-15.
- Γ_D is symmetric to round-off and annihilates translations to ≤ 1e-15 relative. Its λ_min on Range(Π) is positive for every reference.

## 3. Metrics

- **E_Γ** = ‖Γ_h − Γ_ref‖₂/‖Γ_ref‖₂, from dense assembly. Γ_ref = Γ_D everywhere; E against Γ_LF is in `E_G_vsLF`.
- **E_ΓX** = ‖(Γ_h − Γ_ref)X‖_F/‖Γ_ref X‖_F, computed with the matrix-free action. X holds the 10 common Gaussian vectors (fixed seeds 1001–1010, translation removed) of the existing Experiment 1 convention (`run_exp1.action_probes`).
- Also recorded:
  - the per-vector maximum;
  - the 3 slowest eigenvectors of Γ_ref outside the translation space (`E_GX_slow_max`) and the fastest one (`E_GX_fast`);
  - the 3 translation vectors. These are excluded from the relative errors; their absolute residual ‖Γ_h t‖/‖Γ_ref‖ is `mom_opnorm_max`.
- Further columns:
  - symmetry ‖Γ_h − Γ_hᵀ‖_F/‖Γ_h‖_F;
  - λ_min of sym(Γ_h) on Range(Π), the zero-momentum subspace, and its count of negative eigenvalues;
  - `E_G_meshfree`: Γ_S^{rc} with the exact truncated lattice Fourier sum instead of the mesh. This is the truncation-only error.
  - `E_G_mesh_only` = ‖Γ_L^{mesh} − Γ_L^{kc}‖/‖Γ_ref‖;
  - setup and cached matvec times.
- Sweep timings were taken with 3 concurrent single-thread workers. The serial timings are in `results/timing_serial.csv`.

## 4. Results

### Baseline accuracy and what limits it

At the baseline (ξ r_c = 6, k_c/ξ = 12 nominal / 11.967 shell-snapped, M = 80, p = 9, κ = 0.7, ρ = 0.75), over all 9 configurations:

| metric | value |
|---|---|
| E_Γ | **2.1e-14** (max) |
| E_ΓX (Gaussian) | **9.0e-15** |
| E_ΓX, slowest / fastest modes | ≤ 1.0e-14 |
| truncation only (mesh-free) | 2.0e-15 |
| mesh only | 2.1e-14 |
| reference floor | 1.1e-15 |

- **The baseline is mesh-limited.** Its error is 20× the reference floor. The cutoff, M and p panels of Figs. A1/A2 (a)–(d) show that this plateau is the M = 80, p = 9 mesh error, not the reference or round-off. Raising M or p lowers it, while raising ξ r_c beyond about 5.75 or k_c/ξ beyond about 11.5 does not.

### Sweeps 1–2 — real-space and Fourier cutoffs (Figs. A1, A2, panels (a), (b))

Both errors decay as a Gaussian in the cutoff until they reach the baseline mesh plateau (E_Γ 2.1e-14, E_ΓX 9.0e-15). Representative values (maximum over 9 configurations):

| ξ r_c | E_Γ | E_ΓX | k_c/ξ (shell-snapped) | E_Γ | E_ΓX |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | 1.4e-2 | 9.2e-3 | 3.958 | 5.2e-3 | 1.8e-3 |
| 3 | 1.2e-4 | 8.0e-5 | 5.983 | 5.1e-5 | 2.1e-5 |
| 4 | 1.2e-7 | 9.0e-8 | 7.986 | 4.3e-8 | 2.1e-8 |
| 5 | 1.6e-11 | 1.4e-11 | 9.978 | 8.3e-12 | 4.4e-12 |
| ≥ 5.75 | plateau 2.1e-14 | 9.0e-15 | ≥ 11.5 | plateau 2.1e-14 | 9.0e-15 |

- **Fits** (`results/fits.csv`; points above 1e3 × floor):
  - real space, ln E = a − c (ξ r_c)²: c = 0.983 (E_Γ), 0.969 (E_ΓX), for ξ r_c ∈ [2, 5.25];
  - Fourier, ln E = a − c (k_c/ξ)²/4 (the fit is stored in s_F = k_c/(2ξ)): c = 0.975 (E_Γ), 0.947 (E_ΓX), for k_c/ξ ∈ [3.96, 9.98], i.e. 0.244 and 0.237 per (k_c/ξ)².
  - These are close to the leading e^{−(ξ r_c)²} and e^{−(k_c/ξ)²/4} behaviour (c = 1), with algebraic prefactors.
- **Shell rounding** is visible as uneven spacing of the k_c/ξ points; for example k_c/ξ = 4 is realised as 3.958.
- **Coverage:** the Fourier sweep covers k_c/ξ = 3.96–12.95 only; no point below 3.96 was computed.
- **The Fourier error is about 2–3× smaller** than the real-space error at equal dimensionless cutoff (k_c/ξ = 2 ξ r_c).

### Sweep 3 — FFT mesh M (Figs. A1, A2, panel (c))

- **Baseline** (p = 9): every M = 24 … 80 is valid.
  - E_Γ falls from 3.9e-9 (M = 24) to 2.1e-14 (M = 80); E_ΓX from 1.5e-9 to 9.0e-15.
  - The fitted rate is E ∝ M^−10.3 (M = 24–54; for both E_Γ_mesh-only and E_ΓX; `results/fits.csv`, not drawn). At M = 80 the error has still not reached the reference floor.
- **Moderate** (p = 7, s = 4):
  - The mesh error decays as M^−8.25.
  - It falls below the s = 4 truncation level (1.2e-7) at M ≈ 24.
  - From M = 24 to 80, E_Γ saturates at 1.2e-7 = E_G_meshfree (E_ΓX at 8.9e-8). The trend is therefore distinguishable from the floor.

### Configuration families

- Uniform, moderate and strong configurations give **the same errors** at every sweep point: family medians differ by at most 1.27× and individual configurations by at most 1.39×; the curves overlap in Figs. A1 and A2.
- Clustering does not degrade the accuracy of either the real-space or the mesh part at these parameters.

## 5. Conclusions

1. The direct periodic reference is converged and independently confirmed for every κ (0.2–2.0) and ρ (0.3–1.0): certified ≤ 1e-13, measured ≤ 3.3e-15 against LatticeFriction. Weak screening needs R_D = 190; R = 55 would be unconverged for κ < 0.7.
2. Candidate 1 reaches E_Γ ≈ 2e-14 and E_ΓX ≈ 9e-15 at the baseline. That level is set by the M = 80, p = 9 mesh, about 20× the reference floor.
3. The truncation errors follow e^{−c (ξ r_c)²} and e^{−c (k_c/ξ)²/4} with c ≈ 0.95–0.98. The mesh error follows ≈ M^−(p+1), at M^−10.3 for p = 9 and M^−8.3 for p = 7.
4. The accuracy is insensitive to clustering. It is nearly insensitive to ρ and κ at fixed truncation, and the mesh error grows with h = L/M at low density.
5. The complete Γ_h is symmetric and momentum-conserving to 1e-17. It was positive definite on Range(Π) at every tested parameter, including the crudest point of every one-at-a-time sweep (ξ r_c = 2; k_c/ξ = 3.96; M = 24 baseline / 16 moderate; p = 3).
